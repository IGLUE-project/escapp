2. Personal data collected

Escapp may collect and process the following personal data:

  • Data provided through the registration form, including first name, last name, alias, email, password, education level, and user type (student/teacher).
  • Data provided through contact, feedback, and reporting forms, including name, email address, and any information voluntarily submitted.

The personal data collected by Escapp is used for the following purposes:

  • To ensure proper operation and improvement of the platform.

  • To manage the creation and monitoring of escape rooms via the platform.
  • To improve escape rooms and the teaching and learning processes related to them.
  • To receive, review, and manage inquiries, feedback, and reports (e.g., reports of inappropriate content) submitted through the platform.

Users may opt out of receiving these communications at any time by contacting the data controller at the email address provided in this Privacy Policy.

7. Data recipients

Data is securely stored on servers located in the European Union. The data controller will not share personal data with third parties, except as specified below.

Anonymized data may be shared as described in this Privacy Policy.

User aliases may be publicly displayed or made accessible to anyone, including individuals who are not registered on the platform.

The platform allows the owner of an escape room and its collaborators to access each other's personal data, including first name, last name, alias, and email address.

The platform allows the owner of an escape room and its collaborators to access the following personal data of its participants (i.e., users who participate in it):

  • Identifying and demographic data: first name, last name, alias, email, and education level.
  • Automatically collected data on participation and progress in the escape room, including shift registrations, team names and members, attendance, joining and leaving events, attempts at solving puzzles (successful and failed), solved puzzles, hints requested, hints obtained, and grades.
  • Timestamps associated with the actions listed above, including the times at which users register, join or leave the escape room, attempt to solve puzzles (successful or not), and request or receive hints.

The owner of an escape room and its collaborators may use the above data of its participants solely for the following purposes:

  • To manage participation, monitoring, and grading of participants.
  • To improve the escape room and related teaching and learning processes.
  • To conduct research and statistical analysis, provided that participants have given their consent.

Personal data submitted through contact, feedback, and reporting forms may be shared with the intended recipients, including escape room owners, their collaborators, and the Escapp development team, where applicable.

Certain pages and resources provided by Escapp may load fonts from third-party services, such as Google Fonts. When these resources are loaded, technical information, including users' IP addresses, may be transmitted to the corresponding service providers.

Escape rooms may include third-party content and web applications provided by their owners and collaborators. When users access such content or applications, their personal data may be collected and processed by the corresponding providers or developers, in accordance with their own privacy policies, where applicable.

Essential cookies are used to ensure the proper functioning of the web platform. Escapp itself does not use cookies or other technologies for tracking purposes. However, escape rooms may include third-party content or web applications provided by their owners and collaborators, which may use cookies and other tracking technologies. You can find more information about the cookies used by the platform in the Cookie Policy.
