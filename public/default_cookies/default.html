Cookie Policy – Escapp

This Cookie Policy applies to Escapp (https://...), hereinafter referred to as "the platform".

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, mobile phone, etc.) when you visit a website.

2. Types of cookies used by the platform

The platform only uses the following type of cookies:

  • Technical or essential cookies (first-party): essential for the operation of the platform. They do not require your consent.

Although the platform itself does not use cookies or other technologies for tracking purposes, escape rooms may include third-party content or web applications provided by their owners and collaborators, which may use cookies and other tracking technologies.

4. How can you manage cookies?

When accessing the platform, a notice informs you that it only uses essential cookies to ensure its proper functioning. As these cookies do not require your consent, no action is needed to enable their use. You can manage these cookies at any time from your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

We may update this Cookie Policy based on legal or technical changes. We recommend that you review this policy periodically to stay informed about how and why we use cookies. You can always check the date of the last update to this policy on this page.
